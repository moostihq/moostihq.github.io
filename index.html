What is Moosti?

Moosti is an umbrella of small, specialized sites, one per task. The Pomodoro timer that grew into a daily habit became the seed: focused tools that respect your time and your data.

Every tool follows three rules: it runs in your browser, it loads fast, and it does one thing well. We make money through unobtrusive ads. There is no Pro tier, no account, no upload.
