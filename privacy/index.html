Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-04

Your files

All file processing happens locally in your browser. We do not upload, store, or have any access to the files you operate on through Moosti tools.

Information we collect automatically

When you visit Moosti, our servers and third-party analytics services may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Pages visited, referring URLs, and time spent on pages
  • Clickstream data and aggregate usage metrics

This information is used to operate the service, diagnose problems, and improve Moosti.

Cookies and analytics

We use the following cookies and tracking, only after you consent via the banner:

  • Google Analytics 4 — pageviews, tool usage events, aggregate metrics.
  • Google AdSense — ad personalization (you may decline).
  • Strictly necessary — locale preference, consent state, shortcut list. Always set.

Third-party services

We use third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense. These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of data. We are not responsible for the practices of those third parties.

International data transfers

By using the service, you consent to the transfer of automatically collected technical data (such as IP address and analytics data) to servers located in various countries, which may have different data protection laws than your own.

Data retention

We do not maintain user accounts, so there is no account data to retain or delete. Analytics data collected by third parties is governed by their respective retention policies. Consent preferences stored in your browser are kept until you clear them.

Your rights

You can revisit your consent choices at any time via the "Privacy preferences" link in the footer. Because we do not maintain user accounts, we have no personally identifiable data to delete on request. If you believe we hold any personal data about you (e.g. from a support inquiry), contact us and we will address it promptly.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the service. However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of Moosti after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
