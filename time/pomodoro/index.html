How to use the Pomodoro Timer

  1. Pick a task you want to focus on.
  2. Press Start. Work without interruption for 25 minutes.
  3. When the timer rings, take a 5-minute break — stretch, drink water, look away from the screen.
  4. After four focus sessions, take a longer 15-minute break.
  5. Repeat. Most people get four to eight productive sessions per workday.

You can adjust focus length, break length, and how often the long break appears in the Settings panel.

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique was developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s, named after the tomato-shaped kitchen timer he used as a university student. The core idea is simple: split work into fixed intervals (traditionally 25 minutes), separated by short breaks. Each interval is one pomodoro. The structure has two effects: it removes the cognitive cost of “deciding to start”, and it forces regular recovery so attention doesn’t degrade across the day.

Why a client-side Pomodoro?

This timer runs entirely in your browser. There is no server tracking your sessions, no account to create, no analytics on what you’re working on. Your settings are stored locally in your browser and never leave your device. If the network drops, the timer keeps running. That privacy and reliability is the point of every Moosti tool.

Frequently asked questions

Does the timer keep running if I switch tabs? Yes. The timer is driven by wall-clock time, so even if the browser throttles the background tab, the count stays accurate.

Can I get notified when a session ends? Yes — the first time you press Start, your browser will ask permission to send notifications. If you grant it, you’ll get a desktop notification when each session completes. Notifications are local; nothing is sent over the network.

Are my settings synced across devices? No. Settings are stored locally in this browser. We don’t run an account system and we don’t store your data. If you use Pomodoro on a phone and a laptop, you’ll set them independently.

What if I want shorter or longer sessions? Open the Settings panel below the timer and change the Focus minutes. The original technique is 25 minutes, but research suggests anywhere between 20 and 50 minutes works well depending on the task.

Why doesn’t it auto-start the next session? Auto-start is off by default to give you a moment to acknowledge the break. You can turn it on in Settings.

Что такое техника Помодоро?

Техника Помодоро была разработана Франческо Чирилло в конце 1980-х годов. Главная идея проста: разбить работу на фиксированные интервалы (обычно 25 минут), разделённые короткими перерывами. Каждый интервал — один помодоро. Структура имеет два эффекта: устраняет когнитивные затраты на «решение начать» и обеспечивает регулярное восстановление, чтобы внимание не угасало в течение дня.

什么是番茄工作法？

番茄工作法由弗朗切斯科·西里洛于1980年代末开发，以他大学时使用的番茄形厨房计时器命名。核心理念很简单：将工作分成固定的时间段（通常25分钟），中间穿插短暂休息。每个时间段就是一个番茄。这种结构有两个效果：消除”决定开始”的认知成本，并强制定期恢复，防止注意力在一天内衰退。

ポモドーロタイマーの使い方

ポモドーロ・テクニックとは？

ポモドーロ・テクニックは、1980年代後半にフランチェスコ・シリロが開発しました。核心的なアイデアはシンプルです：作業を固定の時間間隔（通常25分）に分割し、短い休憩で区切ります。各間隔が1つのポモドーロです。この構造には2つの効果があります：「始めるかどうか迷う」という認知的コストを排除し、定期的な回復を強制することで、一日を通じて集中力が低下するのを防ぎます。
