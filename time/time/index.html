World Clock

Live digital clock with pinned time zones. See what time it is anywhere in the world.

How to use the World Clock

  1. The main clock shows your local time automatically.
  2. Toggle between 12-hour and 24-hour format with the switch button.
  3. Add time zones by clicking Add zone and selecting a city.
  4. Remove any zone by clicking the × button next to it.
  5. Your pinned zones are saved in your browser and persist across visits.

What is a World Clock?

A world clock lets you see the current time in multiple locations at once. It’s essential for remote teams, travelers, and anyone coordinating across borders. Unlike searching “time in Tokyo” repeatedly, a pinned world clock gives you instant, always-updated reference.

Privacy

This tool runs entirely in your browser. No location data is sent to any server. Time zone calculations use the native Intl API built into your browser.
