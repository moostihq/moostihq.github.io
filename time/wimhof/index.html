How to use the Wim Hof Breathing Timer

  1. Sit or lie down in a comfortable position.
  2. Round 1: Breathe in deeply through the nose or mouth, then let go naturally (don’t force the exhale). Repeat 30–40 times.
  3. After the last exhale, stop breathing and hold for as long as comfortable. The timer tracks your retention.
  4. When you need to breathe, inhale deeply and hold for 15 seconds — this is the recovery breath.
  5. Repeat for 3–4 rounds. Most people notice longer retentions by round 3.

What is the Wim Hof Method?

The Wim Hof Method is a breathing technique developed by Dutch extreme athlete Wim Hof. It combines deep rhythmic breathing, cold exposure, and mindset training to influence the autonomic nervous system. The breathing protocol increases oxygen levels, reduces CO₂, and can produce feelings of euphoria, clarity, and reduced stress.

Why a browser timer?

This timer removes the guesswork — no counting breaths in your head, no checking a clock during retention. It runs entirely in your browser. No audio files to download, no account to create. Just open the page and breathe.

Important notes

  • Never practice while driving, in water, or in any situation where losing consciousness could be dangerous.
  • If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, stop and return to normal breathing.
  • This is a guidance tool, not medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional if you have respiratory or cardiovascular conditions.

O Método Wim Hof é uma técnica de respiração desenvolvida pelo atleta extremo holandês Wim Hof. 如何使用维姆·霍夫呼吸计时器

  1. 以舒适的姿势坐下或躺下。
  2. 第1轮： 通过鼻子或嘴深吸气，然后自然呼出。重复30次。
  3. 最后一次呼气后，停止呼吸，尽可能长时间屏息。计时器记录您的屏息时间。
  4. 需要呼吸时，深吸一口气并屏住15秒 — 这是恢复呼吸。
  5. 重复3-4轮。

ウィム・ホフ呼吸タイマーの使い方
